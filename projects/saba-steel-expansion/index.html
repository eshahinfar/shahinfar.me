SABA Steel Company Expansion

Erection, commissioning and renovation work across the steelmaking plant, thin slab caster and hot strip mill during the expansion of SABA Steel, carried out in association with Danieli.

Scope of work

  • Led renovation projects for Steelmaking, Thin Slab Caster and Hot Strip Mill facilities.
  • Erection, commissioning and modification of hydraulic systems for EAF ladle preheaters.
  • Erection and commissioning of bag filter and wet scrubber dedusting systems.
  • Installation and commissioning of hydraulic systems throughout the steel production line.
  • Supported EAF renovation, teeming and charging crane installation, steel structure installation and process optimization.
  • Thin Slab Caster: mold and segment preparation, oscillator installation, tundish pre-heater installation, renovation and banana-frame adjustment.
  • Hot Strip Mill: descaling pump installation and commissioning and addition of the seventh mill stand.
